Publications

“Remittance Frictions and Seasonal Poverty.”

with Mushfiq Mobarak and Arjun Kharel
Forthcoming, Quantitative Economics  

Remittance frictions interact with seasonal income volatility to generate poverty traps among rural households in Nepal. Using experimental variation in remittance costs, we estimate the effect of reducing frictions on household consumption, savings, and migration decisions.
