About Research CV

I am a PhD student at the Harris School of Public Policy at the University of Chicago. My research focuses on labor markets, seasonal migration, and household welfare in South Asia, with fieldwork based in Nepal. I am an NSF Graduate Research Fellow and Pearson Scholar.

Working Papers

“Remittance Frictions and Seasonal Poverty” with Ahmed Mushfiq Mobarak and Arjun Kharel
Revise and Resubmit, Quantitative Economics  

Remittance frictions interact with seasonal income volatility to generate poverty traps among rural households in Nepal. Using experimental variation in remittance costs, we estimate the effect of reducing frictions on household consumption, savings, and migration decisions.
